# StayEasy Hotels & Stays — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Help Center Version:** 2026.1  
**Category:** Booking, Cancellations & Billing Support  

### Q: What is your general check-in and check-out schedule?
Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM across all StayEasy partner properties. Check-out is by 11:00 AM on your departure morning. If you need an early check-in or late check-out, please reach out directly to the hotel front desk via the app, and our team will do their best to accommodate you based on room readiness.

### Q: What payment methods are supported on the StayEasy platform?
We accept all standard domestic and international payment instruments, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa, Mastercard, RuPay cards, and direct Net Banking across major Indian banking institutions.

### Q: Can I cancel my reservation if my plans change?
Yes! We understand that travel plans can be unpredictable. We offer Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in on standard rates. If you cancel your standard stay more than 24 hours prior to the check-in time, you will receive a full refund with zero cancellation penalties. Please note that bookings marked as non-refundable promotional saver deals are excluded from this benefit.

### Q: How long does it take to receive my refund back into my account?
Once your cancellation request is received and processed in our system, your refund will be returned directly to the original payment method used during checkout. Refunds take up to 14 business days to reflect in your bank account or card statement depending on your banking provider's processing schedule.

### Q: Are meals included in the room price?
Complimentary breakfast is included if your booking rate plan explicitly indicates 'Breakfast Included'. For room-only plans, continental and hot breakfast spreads can be purchased directly at the on-site dining area for ₹350 plus taxes per guest.

### Q: Is Wi-Fi complimentary at StayEasy hotels?
High-speed wireless internet access is provided free of charge to all registered guests across rooms, business centers, and common lobby areas.

### Q: What should I bring when checking in?
Please bring a valid Government photo ID for every adult guest (such as Aadhaar, Voter Card, Passport, or Driving License) along with your digital booking voucher or booking confirmation SMS.

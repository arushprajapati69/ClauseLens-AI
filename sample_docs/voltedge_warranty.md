# VoltEdge Technologies — Limited Consumer Hardware Warranty
**Product Family:** VoltEdge Wireless Audio & True Wireless Stereo (TWS) Earbuds  
**Policy Document:** VE-WARR-REV5  

## 1. Scope & Duration of Limited Warranty
VoltEdge Technologies Private Limited ("VoltEdge") warrants that the authentic VoltEdge hardware product ("Product") purchased from an authorized retail channel or official online store is free from manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal consumer use.
- **Main Hardware & Charging Case:** Covered for a period of one (1) year from the original date of purchase as recorded on the official tax invoice.
- **Internal Rechargeable Batteries:** Internal lithium polymer battery cells and charging case power cells are covered for a limited period of six (6) months from the original date of purchase, given normal degradation inherent in chemical battery life.
- **Included Cables & Ear Tips:** Bundled USB-C charging cables and silicone ear tip accessories are covered for ninety (90) days against out-of-the-box defects.

## 2. Mandatory Eligibility Conditions & Claim Procedures
To qualify for warranty evaluation, repair, or replacement under this Policy, the claimant must satisfy all of the following requirements:
- **Proof of Purchase:** A valid original tax invoice, electronic receipt, or order confirmation from an authorized VoltEdge vendor clearly showing the purchase date, product serial number, and seller GSTIN is mandatory.
- **Timely Filing Notice:** A warranty claim must be filed within thirty (30) days of discovering a defect. Late claims submitted beyond thirty (30) days of the fault appearing will be disqualified.
- **Original Serial Identifier:** The serial number imprinted on the underside of the charging cradle or inside the earbud stem must remain legible, untampered, and unremoved.

## 3. Explicit Exclusions & Disqualifications
This Limited Warranty strictly excludes and does not cover any damage or failure caused by:
- **Liquid & Moisture Exposure:** Liquid damage resulting from water immersion, high-pressure jets, saltwater, sweat ingress beyond rated IPX standards, or rain exposure is strictly excluded.
- **Physical & Accidental Abuse:** Physical or accidental damage, including dropped units, crushed casings, cracked driver housings, severed cables, or cosmetic scratches resulting from mishandling.
- **Unauthorized Modification & Repair:** Any product opened, repaired, altered, or serviced by an unauthorized third-party technician or independent repair shop.
- **Non-Standard Charging:** Damage caused by operating with incorrect electrical voltage, non-certified wall adapters exceeding 5V/2A, or power surges.

## 4. Warranty Fulfillment Remedies
If a defect arises and a valid claim is received within the applicable Warranty Period, VoltEdge will, at its sole discretion:
1. Repair the hardware defect using new or factory-refurbished certified replacement parts at no charge.
2. Replace the defective unit with an identical model or an updated equivalent model if the original model has reached end-of-life.
Replacement parts or products assume the remaining warranty balance of the original product or thirty (30) days, whichever period is longer.

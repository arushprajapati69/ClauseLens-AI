# ClipStream Digital Media — Subscription & Billing Terms
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026  
**Document Reference:** CS-SUB-2026-B  

## 1. Subscription Plans & Access Tiers
ClipStream provides on-demand streaming of original web series, licensed feature films, and live sports broadcasting across supported personal computers, smart televisions, and mobile handheld devices. Access is granted on a recurring subscription basis under either a Monthly Plan or an Annual Premium Plan.

## 2. Promotional Trials & Trial Billing Rules
2.1 **Trial Duration:** Eligible first-time subscribers may enroll in a seven (7) day free trial to experience the service without upfront charges. A valid payment card must be registered upon trial signup for authentication purposes.  
2.2 **Automatic Renewal Conversion:** Your trial auto-renews at ₹499 per month unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Upon expiration of the 7-day trial period, your registered payment method will be immediately billed the standard monthly recurring tariff of ₹499.  
2.3 **Trial Cancellation Steps:** You may terminate your trial at any time through Account Settings > Billing & Subscriptions > Cancel Membership.

## 3. Recurring Billing Cycles & Cancellation Rules
3.1 **Monthly Subscription Billing:** Monthly subscriptions recur automatically on the numerical calendar day corresponding to your original billing start date.  
3.2 **No Partial Month Refunds:** When you cancel a monthly subscription, membership access remains active through the end of your current monthly billing period. We provide no refunds for partial months or unused watch hours.  
3.3 **Annual Plan Refund Window:** Annual subscriptions are eligible for a full refund if requested within a fourteen (14) day refund window starting from the initial purchase date, provided total stream playback time on the account does not exceed three (3) hours.

## 4. Payment Delinquency & Account Suspension
If a recurring charge fails due to insufficient funds or an expired payment card, ClipStream will retry the transaction over a five-day grace period. If payment remains outstanding, account access will be suspended until payment information is updated.

## 5. Changes to Tariffs and Service Features
ClipStream reserves the right to modify subscription pricing upon providing at least thirty (30) days prior written notice via email to registered account holders. Continued use of the platform after the effective price change constitutes acceptance of the new billing amount.
